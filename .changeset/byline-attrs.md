`val.attrs(post, "author")` tags an element with one field of what it shows. Use it for a value Val never encodes, like an `s.keyOf` key, a date or a colour. A byline that shows the author a post points at can carry the post's `author` field, so clicking it in the Studio opens the choice of author.

On a phone, a tour step about Pages, Media, Data or Settings points at the menu button, which is where they are when the rail is not drawn.

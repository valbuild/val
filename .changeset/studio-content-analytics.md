Val Studio now shows content analytics beside the content being edited, for a hosted project with content analytics switched on. Each field and list row shows how many page views it was seen in (hover for how many it was on the page in, and the share). The heading of what is open says the same for all of it, and lists what in it was seen most, by the names the Studio gives them everywhere else. Nothing is shown, and nothing is asked of Val Build, for a project with analytics off or a Studio not signed in to Val Build.

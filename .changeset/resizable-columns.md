The workspace's columns can be resized: drag the edge of Content, the Sketchpad or the Assistant, and the preview takes the rest. A drag stops before the preview gets too narrow to read a page in, so nothing folds away while you drag. The arrow keys move a focused edge too, and double-clicking an edge puts the column back to its usual width. The widths are remembered in your browser.

The assistant's chats in a proposal are shared with everyone on the project. A chat started while you are in a proposal belongs to it: anyone on the project who opens the proposal sees it in the chat list, with who is in it, and can read it and keep asking. Messages are named once more than one person has written, and the chat header has a link to the chat. One question runs at a time: if someone else's question is running, what you write is sent when its answer is done. Chats started on the site stay your own.

It needs the content server that knows about proposal chats; with an older one, chats work as before.

In a proposal, **Compare with the site** can now show a changed page as a page: pick a page and switch from **Fields** to **Page** to see the page as the site serves it beside the page as the proposal does, unsaved changes included, at desktop or phone width. Each side has an **Open** link to see it in a tab of its own. A page the proposal adds or removes says so on the side it is not on, rather than loading a 404.

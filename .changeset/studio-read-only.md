New: when a project's organization on Val Build is read-only, the Studio says so above the editor before anyone starts typing. An organization becomes read-only when its trial has ended, or when a payment failed and the grace period has run out.

The site keeps running, but nothing can be saved or published until the plan is fixed. An owner gets a button to Billing (**Start Pro** or **Update payment**). Everyone else sees who the owners are and gets a button to email them.

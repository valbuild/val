A click on the page brings Content back when it was switched off or folded

With Select on, a click on the page opens the field in Content. When Content had been switched off from the top bar, or folded to a strip to make room, the field opened in a column you could not see, so the click seemed to do nothing. Content now comes back with the field in it, and the caret in the field.

**Select & edit** puts the caret in the Studio when it cannot put it on the page

Clicking a text on the page that cannot be typed in where it is (a name inside a sentence, say) opens its field in Content as before, and now also puts the caret in that field, so you can start typing straight away.

A click on the page also brings Content back when it was switched off from the top bar or folded to a strip. It used to open the field in a column you could not see, so the click seemed to do nothing.

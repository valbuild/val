The menu Val draws over your site has been simplified and can now be moved anywhere:

- **Drag it to any edge, on desktop or on a phone.** Let go and it snaps to the nearest edge of the screen, staying as far along that edge as you left it. It snaps to the exact middle when you drop it near the middle. Where you put it is remembered, and existing positions carry over.
- **Fewer buttons.** The bar now has select-to-edit, publish, Studio and (where enabled) the assistant. Everything else is under **⋯**: position (eight presets, if you would rather pick than drag), light or dark mode, **Exit preview mode** and **Hide Val**. The separate "review changes" button is gone. The count of pending changes and errors is now on the Studio button.
- **Smoother animation.** It opens and closes as a single pill growing from its circle. It no longer pauses and then snaps shut, the circle no longer turns oval, and it no longer jumps between two bars when preview mode changes.
